## Context
All systems share `CUserCommand` inside `CCSGOInput::CreateMove[5]` while `SendMove` isolates local camera viewangles. Hardcoded RVAs in `shot_events.cpp` broke on `client.dll` updates, subtick injection on every ground tick broke BunnyHop, 300 Hz render-rate `CreateMove` calls corrupted tick-rate timers and 16-slot histories, and `m_flOldSimulationTime` is absent in current CS2 schemas.

## Decisions
1. **BunnyHop & Edge Jump Arbitration**: Latch BunnyHop per `cmd->command_number` via `step_bhop` (inject `k_in_jump` subtick press at `when = 0.0f` and `up_down` on landing, release for 1 tick if still grounded to re-arm, and keep `k_in_jump` `up` while airborne). Skip `edge_jump` whenever `bhop` is active and `jump_held` is true (or on ladder/noclip/observer move types).
2. **AutoStrafe, Quantized Ground Move, Fire-Tick Movement & Slide Walk**: Use `k_air_dot_target = 0.0f` above `24.4 u/s` in `compute_autostrafe` for AirAccelerate optimum, and run `compute_sector_matched_autostrafe` after `AntiAim` relative to `ref_yaw` (`aa_angles.yaw` when `aa_gate`, else `camera_view.yaw`), latching `strafe_sign` once per `command_number` and keeping `|yaw_delta(ref_yaw, steer_yaw)| <= 22.5 deg` with zero spin. On ground under `AntiAim`, replace 45-degree AA-yaw snapping (`align_aa_yaw_for_ground_move`) with `compute_quantized_ground_move` (`{-1, 0, +1}` 2D world-space error diffusion) preserving arbitrary `aa_angles.yaw`. On `will_fire` and `on_shot` ticks, re-solve movement into `base_cmd->viewangles->y` via `rotate_move_inputs` (preserving `strafe_out` in air or counter-strafe when `!stop_dec.zero_move`) or `compute_quantized_ground_move`, always call `sync_move_held_buttons`, and always call `clear_subtick_view_deltas` / `strip_subtick_buttons` when angles or movement rotate (including WASD release ticks). When `leg_movement` (`Slide walk`) is enabled on ground while walking and not stopping/shooting/jumping/crouching, invert directional `held` button bits while preserving `forwardmove`/`leftmove`.
3. **Anti-Aim**: Unify Anti-Aim in `apply_antiaim_full` with strict feature independence (`at_targets` selects `enemy_yaw` vs `camera_view.yaw` only; `base_yaw::off` applies `ref_yaw + yaw_offset` without hidden `+180`; `fake_static`/`jitter`/`random` apply `+180` + mode offset seeded deterministically on `cmd->command_number`; `freestanding` overrides base yaw only when corner-tucked via `freestanding_head_probe`, `freestanding_trace_target` backing off from enemy pawn to prevent self-hits, and `select_freestanding_yaw` hysteresis; `auto_adjust` applies `pose_head_yaw_offset` weapon-type/group & pitch-down `[35, 89]` deg compensation across `fake_static`/`jitter`/`random`). Gate Anti-Aim via `bound_feature::anti_aim`, and when `!on_move` suppress whenever `speed_2d > 5.0f || has_move_input`.
4. **RageBot UI Balance**: Move `Lag compensation` (`backtrack` + `backtrack_ms` + `extrapolation_ms`) cleanly into the right column or compact the cards so no `RageBot` control clips vertically at `1000x650`.
5. **Runtime Cleanups**: Pass `client_size` into `update_shot_events` and validate manager/event vtable pointers lie inside `[client_base, client_base + client_size)` instead of hardcoded stale RVAs; fix `bind_state::sample` Hold state; expose `Anti-Aim` and `Peek assist` keybinds in `page_settings.cpp` and `esp.cpp`; delete duplicate helper functions in `world.cpp`; reset movement/lagcomp/backtrack state on disconnect, menu open, and focus loss.
6. **Ragebot, Lagcomp, Backtrack, AutoStop & Subtick Hygiene**: Isolate `arms` (`group 5`) from `pelvis` (`group 3`), wire `w_cfg.ignore_limbs`, and implement exact OBB `box` intersection + `hitboxes.cpp` Fallback #2 (`0x1D4`/`0x1D0` bounds & atomic clear). Isolate `chance_multi` head/torso/limb groups and wire `safe_point` + `strict_hitchance`. Detect on-shot lagcomp records via `last_shot_time` (`(prev_sim_time, sim_time]`) without `m_flOldSimulationTime`, validate `sv_maxunlag` windows cleanly, and retain up to 3 distinct records `[newest, shooting, oldest]`. Deduplicate `backtrack_history::push` at 300 Hz and reset on `!in_game`. Gate extrapolation strictly on `tick_gap >= 2` (`compute_extrapolation_gap_ms`) and align `render_tick_count` without `+ 1`. Honor `prefer_body_aim` and break only after `eval_hc` confirms lethal hitchance. Keep `AutoStop` engaged on firing ticks, wire `auto_stop_speed`/`auto_stop_ticks`/`auto_stop_wait` + `is_between_shots_window`, honor `cfg.rage.remove_recoil`, check `cs_pawn_resume_zoom` & unscoped HC in `auto_scope`, clear `k_in_attack2`/`attack2_start_history_index` on primary `auto_revolver` shots, guard `active_wep.range > 10.0f` and `imp.hitgroup`/helmet/shotgun scaling, map Zeus (`def_index == 31`) to pistol group, advance `cycle_cursor` only on fire ticks, cache redundant scans within the same `command_number`, tick `auto_pistol_wait` and `peek_assist.stall_ticks` once per `command_number`, and guard `RepeatedPtrField` `capacity`/`ss->max`/`nullptr` plus `0x60u` `has_bits` in `subtick_move_step_pb`.
